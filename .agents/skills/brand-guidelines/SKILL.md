# Brand Guidelines — La Libertad Avanza (Almirante Brown)

Official brand system for `llabrown-web`, strictly aligned with the national institutional identity of **La Libertad Avanza** (`https://www.lalibertadavanza.com.ar/`).

## 1. Official Color System

### Core Institutional Palette
- **Violeta Institucional (Pantone 2627 C)**: `#371859` — Primary brand surface, headers, primary buttons, institutional authority.
- **Violeta Profundo**: `#230552` / `#2f0163` — Hero backgrounds, footer, high-contrast dark surfaces.
- **Violeta Medio / Hover**: `#4c2882` / `#6c4c99` — Interactive states, subtle borders on dark, secondary badges.
- **Celeste Argentino (Acento Oficial)**: `#39b5e6` (`#0ea5e9` on light backgrounds where AA contrast is needed) — Institutional accent, active indicators, secondary highlights, patriotic link accents.
- **Charcoal Neutro**: `#1d1d1d` — Primary reading text on light backgrounds (never pure `#000000`).
- **Superficie Clara**: `#f5f4f8` / `#ffffff` — Clean editorial sections with generous breathing room.

### Strictly Forbidden Colors (Zero Tolerance)
- **NO yellow, gold, or amber** anywhere in UI, icons, badges, gradients, or GeoJSON map layers (`amber-*`, `yellow-*`, `gold`, `#fbbf24`, `#f59e0b`, `#eab308`, `#d4af37`). Yellow/gold visually evokes PRO / Cambiemos and dilutes LLA's violet-white-celeste identity.

## 2. Typography & Hierarchy

- **Display & Headings**: `Montserrat` — Weight `700`/`800`, tight tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.03em`), line-height `1.06`–`1.18` (official geometric sans-serif of La Libertad Avanza Nacional).
- **Body & UI Controls**: `Montserrat` — Weight `400`/`500`/`600`, comfortable reading measure (`45ch`–`65ch`), line-height `1.55`–`1.7`.
- **Anti-AI Typography Rules**:
  - Avoid putting random single words in italic gradient colors inside every heading.
  - Only use numbered markers (`01`, `02`, `03`) when describing a genuine chronological step-by-step sequence (such as how a neighbor's claim is processed).
  - Prefer sentence case on buttons and form labels; reserve uppercase tracking strictly for short institutional eyebrows (e.g., `HCD Almirante Brown`).
