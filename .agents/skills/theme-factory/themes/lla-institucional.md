# LLA Institucional (Violeta & Celeste)

Official institutional theme inspired by La Libertad Avanza Nacional (`lalibertadavanza.com.ar`), combining deep Pantone 2627 C violet authority, crisp white editorial space, and Argentine celeste accents—with zero yellow or gold tones.

## Color Palette

- **Primary Violet (Pantone 2627 C)**: `#371859` — Primary buttons, institutional headers, key brand blocks
- **Deep Violet**: `#230552` — Hero and footer dark canvas
- **Soft Violet**: `#6c4c99` — Subtle dark-surface borders and secondary tags
- **Argentine Celeste**: `#39b5e6` — Active highlights, data accents, interactive badges
- **Charcoal Ink**: `#1d1d1d` — Primary body typography on light backgrounds
- **Cool Slate**: `#52525b` — Secondary captions and metadata
- **Light Surface**: `#f5f4f8` — Alternating clean section background
- **Pure White**: `#ffffff` — Cards, forms, and primary reading surfaces

## Typography

- **Headers**: Montserrat (700 / 800, tracking `-0.03em`)
- **Body Text**: Montserrat (400 / 500 / 600, line-height `1.6`)

## Best Used For

Institutional portals, legislative transparency dashboards, citizen claim platforms, territorial maps, and official communication pieces for La Libertad Avanza — Almirante Brown.
